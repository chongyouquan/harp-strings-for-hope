A movement of music, healing, and giving forward

Harp Strings for Hope is a movement of music, healing, and giving forward, a living tapestry of songs, videos, and multimedia creations born of love for the Earth and the spirit of renewal.

Each melody, word, and image is created to kindle a song of hope upon the heart strings of all who listen and watch, awakening compassion, unity, inclusiveness, and care for the natural world, while extending support to those navigating health challenges, grief and loss, and life transitions.

This campaign is to raise the seed funding for the creation of the Harp Strings for Hope multimedia fundraising platform, the foundation upon which the songs, videos, and creative offerings will live and grow. The platform will serve as both a musical sanctuary and a giving portal, a place where people can experience and order the music and videos, then choose, through a simple dropdown menu, where to pay them forward. It will connect art and service, allowing each creative work to become a vessel of generosity, supporting organizations, projects, and individuals devoted to healing, sustainability, and the wellbeing of all life.

Through this platform of giving and art, together we can uplift lives, honor the sacred waters, and spread ripples of peace, freedom, sustainability, unity, diversity, and inclusiveness throughout our shared human family.

It is a vessel to empower healing campaigns and creative projects that honor Mother Earth and her inhabitants, the two-legged, four-legged, winged, and one-legged ones, the plants and trees, and all beings of land, water, and sky who share this luminous home.

One song, one vision, and one act of kindness at a time, Harp Strings for Hope invites us to remember that beauty can heal, generosity can renew, and music can carry the light of love farther than we can imagine.

    The Vision

    The messages that seemed to arise naturally, woven through songs, children’s books, poems, and other writings, have always spoken of earth stewardship, healing, peace, compassionate communication, sharing from the heart, spreading the light, lighting the lantern of faith, caring for nature and one another, unity and diversity, and other themes that remind us of the goodness and beauty within life.

    The platform will have a dropdown menu that people can use to pay forward to organizations that are helping the Earth and the wellbeing of her inhabitants: humans, animals, plants and trees, natural resources, ocean protection, and coral reef sustainability (the lungs of the ocean), and of course the trees, the lungs of the planet. It will also include organizations standing for giving, peace, freedom, sustainability, unity, diversity, and advocacy for the natural world.

    This vision and body of work is intended to kindle a song of hope upon the heart strings of those connected to the music and to the calling of this Earth, championing human values of sustainability, peace, freedom, unity, and diversity, and celebrating our shared responsibility for the caretaking of Mother Earth, as well as nurturing and empowering conscious, co-creative, and collaborative models of stewardship of her gifts and resources.

    Through these songs and the giving that flows from them, the wish is that each heart be rekindled with courage, compassion, and a renewed sense of belonging to one another and to this living world.

    Expanding the Vision

    Over time, I’ve expanded this vision to include individuals, to help raise funds needed for their healing journeys, as well as other needs and eco-friendly, sustainable projects, so that it can be a vessel of blessing and empowerment for both individuals and organizations seeking to create positive change.

    It is my wish for Harp Strings for Hope to be a lighthouse of inspiration on the shores in times of uncertainty, and a way to connect with other kindred souls and resonant organizations whose divine purpose and mission play our heart strings, strumming chords of remembrance of what is dear to our hearts. Our vision and intention is to be a vessel to help bring harmony, peace, and wellbeing to all beings that share this precious Earth.

    An intention is to increase the reach of so much goodness already being cultivated: giving loving and wonderful ideas the support to spread their wings even farther, so that the ripples can go further, the imprints upon the sands of time, the songs, the outreach.

    Metaphorically speaking, it would be the ripple of a drop of water and the rings going out farther, or the singing tree rings and the mycelium of trees, the nurturance, renewal, and resources to expand the impact and reach of those organizations and individuals who are making a positive difference, and of kindred spirits needing assistance with health, with losing homes in fires, and with challenging life transitions.

    The Springs and the Water Mission

    The inspiration for water advocacy has long been alive within me, yet it was beautifully renewed and rekindled during a water blessing ceremony, a celebration for the discovery of new thermal springs after a fire had cleared away the blackberry overgrowth. The springs became a living symbol of hope and renewal, the rising of the wings of the phoenix from the ashes, a silver lining in the storm clouds of such an intense event.

    This experience deepened my dedication to the mission of honoring and protecting the waters, to help restore balance to the living waters of the Earth through education, conservation, and conscious action. It also reawakened a call within me to make the stewardship of water a central part of the Harp Strings for Hope mission, to help provide clean water to those in need, and to celebrate the sacredness of this life-giving element through music and giving.

    I see life-giving water as the amniotic fluid of the Sacred Feminine, the cradle of life itself. Just as the springs rose from the ashes and the wings of the phoenix unfurled in radiant renewal, this project is about helping new life, hope, and beauty rise from hardship, and letting that renewal flow outward through song, compassion, and service.
